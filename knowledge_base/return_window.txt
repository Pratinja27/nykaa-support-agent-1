Nykaa accepts returns based on the product type, not a single store-wide day count. Beauty that stays sealed can come back within 7 days of delivery, while apparel and footwear get 15 days if tags and hygiene stickers are intact. Electronics and home appliances follow a 10-day doorstep return window only when the box and serial seal are unused. Opened innerwear, earrings and personal-care tips are not taken back once the hygiene seal is broken.
